# Lead-Gen VSL (Loom) — AI Outbound Engine

**Link:** https://www.loom.com/share/66b3d9799bf6409fb25f45ef88dec54c
**Length:** ~5 minutes.
**Who it speaks to:** B2B companies that sell to other businesses and need more qualified meetings — B2B SaaS, consulting agencies, branding/creative agencies, and similar B2B service firms.
**Solution it sells:** AI-Automated Outbound Campaigns (Solutions Catalog #1), i.e. we run lead generation for them.

**Send it when** the diagnosed solution is the outbound lead-gen engine and the lead is a B2B firm that sells to businesses. This is the counterpart to the AI-secretary VSL, which is for home-services installers and does not apply to a lead-gen prospect. Do not send both, and never send a video as a generic attachment — same rule as always, one precise fit or no video at all.

**What the video promises the viewer:** it walks through the whole engine end to end, explains the pricing model, and gives them enough to run outbound themselves even if they never work with us. That "you'll get value either way" framing is the reason it's an easy watch, so it's fair to say that when you send it.

**Commercial terms it states:** the video says we work on performance and charge only when we find a qualified lead, so nothing delivered means nothing paid.

**Careful here — the video states one version of the deal, not the only one.** In practice some clients are billed per qualified lead and some per booked meeting, depending on their business and what's agreed on the call. So in your own writing state the principle (they pay on results, not a retainer, and nothing if we don't deliver) and leave the unit alone — don't write "per meeting" or "per lead" as if it were settled, and never a price, rate or ballpark. If the lead saw the video and quotes it back, confirm the principle rather than correcting them, and take the structure to the call.
