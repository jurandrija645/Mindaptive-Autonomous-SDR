## The company

AeroDefense was founded in 2015 and focuses entirely on drone detection.
**AirWarden** is the brand name for its suite of drone detection systems. The
company's function is airspace awareness: detecting, tracking and locating both
drones **and their pilots**, so security teams can respond effectively.

The founding motivation was a run of 2015 incidents — drones interfering with
firefighting in California, a crash in the stands at the US Open, and a landing
near Angela Merkel.

- **Mission:** affordable, reliable, scalable drone detection for any
  security-conscious organization.
- **US-based:** developed and manufactured in the US, headquartered in
  Oceanport, NJ, which keeps data and hardware supply secure.
- **Legal by design:** detection is *passive*. AirWarden listens for drone
  signals without interfering with or intercepting private drone communications,
  so it operates with no legal waivers or special permissions required.
- **Deployed since 2017/2018** across public safety, correctional facilities,
  stadiums, critical infrastructure and defense — including MetLife Stadium
  commercially since 2018, and proven in hard RF environments like downtown
  Manhattan and Times Square during large outdoor gatherings.

### Credentials worth citing

- Three US Air Force SBIR contracts to accelerate dual-use drone detection.
- Five US patents plus one in Canada.
- First drone detection solution to receive the Department of Homeland
  Security's SAFETY Act **Developmental Testing and Evaluation** designation
  (2019), and awarded **full SAFETY Act Designation** in 2024.

## The AirWarden product line

Match the customer's investment to their level of risk.

**AirWarden Essentials** — cost-effective, wide-area drone and pilot detection,
built for quick deployment.
- Leverages the FAA Remote ID mandate (drones must broadcast identifying
  information and location — effectively a digital licence plate), so it detects
  Remote ID-compliant drones.
- Sold as a flexible annual subscription. The receiver hardware is included with
  the subscription at no separate charge.
- Installation is genuinely quick: mount the small receiver on a building or
  pole and plug it in — roughly five minutes, full deployment in minutes.
- Connects automatically to the cloud console over LTE or ethernet. No ongoing
  maintenance.
- Suits general businesses and government agencies, permanent or temporary —
  large public gatherings, concerts, disaster response.

**AirWarden Spectrum** — multiple layers of advanced frequency-based detection,
designed to catch both FAA Remote ID-compliant **and** non-compliant drones.

**AirWarden Classic** — the more advanced option for detecting non-compliant or
modified drones.

## Platform features

Secure, cloud-based monitoring built on the DoD's Collaborative Drone Detection
Network, which allows information sharing between users and organizations.

- **Real-time tracking and alerts.** Live detection map showing drone and pilot
  activity as it happens. Automated alerts by text and email carrying actionable
  intelligence: the exact GPS locations of both drone and pilot.
- **Detection range** spanning roughly 2–30km depending on configuration.
- **Customizable alert zones** — Reporting, Early Warning and Perimeter zones so
  the response matches the threat.
- **Historical data and reporting.** Replay past incidents and flight paths.
  Charts, graphs and heat maps surface patterns and trends for evidence
  collection, training and proactive security planning. Reports export to PDF
  and CSV.
- **Regional monitoring.** Multiple locations — even across states or regions —
  from a single interface.

## Integrations

Open platform with a flexible architecture supporting APIs, data sharing and
real-time synchronization:

- **UTM (Uncrewed Traffic Management):** feeds real-time drone data into UTM
  networks to improve coordination between air traffic and drone operators.
- **Radar:** catches drones that emit no electronic signature, covering stealth
  or low-profile aircraft.
- **Cameras:** can begin recording automatically the moment a drone is detected.

## Recommended incident response

AeroDefense provides the data; it does not shoot drones down. Forcible
mitigation is dangerous — the drone's behaviour isn't guaranteed and it can
injure people or damage property. The recommended procedure is:

1. Send a security person to the pilot's location.
2. Inform the pilot they are violating regulations and that the system has
   recorded their drone ID.
3. Ask them to land the drone.
4. If they refuse, hand the system's data to law enforcement or the FAA liaison.

The pilot risks criminal charges, FAA fines for unsafe flying, and loss of their
pilot's licence. This data is what makes legal action possible for stalking,
espionage or "peeping tom" activity.
