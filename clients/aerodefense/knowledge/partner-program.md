# AeroDefense Partner Program

Relevant when the lead is a security integrator, reseller, consultant or anyone
who would sell AirWarden on to their own clients rather than deploy it
themselves.

Framing: rising drone activity across sectors is driving strong demand for
detection, with the market projected to grow at over 20% CAGR (MarketsAndMarkets).
Partners get a proven, in-demand product to add to their portfolio.

## Two tracks

**Referral Program** — the no-effort option. The partner makes the introduction
and earns a fee on the resulting customer, paid once AeroDefense receives
customer payment. Suits organizations that want revenue without handling sales
or implementation.

**Reseller / Integrator Program** — the partner purchases from AeroDefense at a
partner discount off MSRP and sells at MSRP or negotiates their own pricing with
the end customer. The discount improves with sales volume. Suits partners who
want full control of the sale and the customer relationship.

> **Never state the referral percentage, the reseller discount, MSRP, or any
> other number in an email.** Describe the two tracks qualitatively — "our
> highest partner margin", "a referral fee when the deal closes" — exactly as the
> templates do, and take the specifics to the call.

## Becoming a partner

1. **Application** — submitted via an AeroDefense representative or
   Sales@AeroDefense.tech.
2. **Approval** — AeroDefense reviews for alignment with its standards and
   market focus, usually including a call about the partner's business and goals.
3. **Agreement** — a partner agreement covering program type, pricing and terms.

## What partners get

- Access to the Knowledge Base, sales training and enablement tools, and
  marketing resources.
- Technical support: installation guidance, technical documentation and
  troubleshooting.
- Sales and marketing assistance: marketing materials, demo support and
  lead-sharing options.
- Recognition as an official AeroDefense partner in marketing and promotional
  materials.

## Installation

AeroDefense systems are designed to be customer-installable, which is a genuine
selling point for partners — no truck roll, no specialist install team.
