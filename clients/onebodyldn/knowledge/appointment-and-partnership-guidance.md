# OneBodyLDN: appointment changes and collaboration enquiries

Source: Mia's instruction 2026-11-11 and Kurt's message shown by Mia, dated
2026-11-10. This file records client guidance for future review and drafting.

## Existing conversations

Continue the existing email thread from its existing sender mailbox. Verify the
actual recipient and any relevant Cc. Do not start a new email or a new subject
for an existing conversation. Do not infer that a reply guarantees inbox placement.

## Changing an appointment

Kurt says appointments are self-service: the person logs into their online
account. TM3 does not directly move an appointment; they must cancel and
rebook. The introductory code is single-use; according to Kurt, cancellation
means they cannot then use it to obtain another free session. If replying,
explain that consequence before they decide to cancel. Do not offer to move the
booking for them, promise another free session, or imply a replacement code
restores eligibility. Kurt also left the option of not replying to Mia's
judgment; do not treat non-response as a universal rule. Account access
problems, disputed charges, and already-cancelled bookings require checking
their actual circumstances; never claim a cancellation or refund occurred.

## Collaboration enquiries

Mia's instruction: tell people asking about collaboration that they can attend
a session and discuss the opportunity there. Do not propose calls, new
commercial terms, or an alternative process in the reply. When providing a new
booking handover, use the current offer-specific partnership link, code, fee,
and sharing terms from booking-options.md, subject to eligibility. If the link
was already provided and remains current, refer to it in the thread.
