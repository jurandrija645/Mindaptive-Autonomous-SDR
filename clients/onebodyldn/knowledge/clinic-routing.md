## How to use the map in replies

1. Check the latest conversation for the lead's preferred area, rejected
   clinics and offer type. An excluded clinic always needs a replacement.
2. Briefly acknowledge the change: "Free sessions are no longer available
   at [old clinic]. Would [eligible alternative] work for you?"
3. Start with a nearby eligible option; when similarly convenient, favour
   a priority clinic. If the lead has supplied a different home/work area,
   use that rather than blindly mapping from the old clinic.
4. Keep the original offer type and duration. Do not turn a 45-minute
   massage into a 20-minute one to use a closer clinic without their choice.
   Victoria, Clapham, Hammersmith, Fulham, Islington, Camden, Kings Cross
   and Elephant and Castle are not on the current 45-minute massage list.
5. If no option suits, ask which area works or say you will confirm an
   alternative. Never promise a live slot, reopen an excluded clinic, or
   infer free-offer eligibility from the public website.
6. If handing over a link/code, retain the matching partnership link,
   VIP8401, the £4.99 booking-fee disclosure and the company-specific
   colleague-sharing sentence from `booking-options.md`.
